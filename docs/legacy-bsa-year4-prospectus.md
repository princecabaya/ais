# Legacy BS Agriculture (BSA) — final fourth-year cohort

**Source:** Palawan State University, External Campuses, Bachelor of Science in Agriculture prospectus, https://www.scribd.com/document/463292395/BSA-PROSPECTUS (fourth-year section).

**Implementation scope:** Only a single legacy BSA Year 4 block for AY 2026–2027 (`BSA-4`), with six first-semester and two second-semester courses. No BSA Years 1–3 are offered or created. Existing BEEd and BSE blocks remain unchanged. Switching to a future academic year does not clone the legacy BSA block.

| Semester | Code | Course title | Total units | Prerequisite |
|---|---|---|---:|---|
| 1 | MC11-AEM4 | Agricultural Economics and Financial Management for Agri-based Enterprise | 3 | CC22 |
| 1 | MC12-AEC2 | Management of Extension Program | 3 (2 lecture + 1 laboratory) | CC21 |
| 1 | PSC | Palawan Studies and Culture | 3 | — |
| 1 | CC23-AEM3 | Principles of Agricultural Entrepreneurship and Marketing | 3 | CC22 |
| 1 | THESIS3 | Manuscript and Defense | 4 | THESIS2 |
| 1 | CC24-SEM2 | Seminar B | 1 | CC18 |
| 2 | CC25-CLQM | Colloquium | 1 | THESIS3 |
| 2 | CC26-ICA | Integrating Courses in Agriculture | 3 | Fourth-year standing |

**Caveats for campus validation:**
- This is the publicly shared PSU External Campuses prospectus; campus staff should check it against their approved BSA curriculum and any remaining student's deficiencies before final class assignments.
- No lower-year BSA offerings have been added.
- Lecture/laboratory contact-hour split is explicitly shown for MC12-AEC2; other special-unit classes (Seminar, Thesis, Colloquium) can be requested without a tentative room/time pending administrator confirmation.
- The active academic-year cohort and curriculum mapping are modeled as a single fourth-year block. If the residual cohort has multiple distinct sections, these should be added after staff verification.
- Administrative affiliation **BSA** is available for legacy faculty. This does not reopen admissions into the dissolved program.
